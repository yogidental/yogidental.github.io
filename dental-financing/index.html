Easy Financing and Insurance Options at Yogi Dental Center

Affordable Dental Care in Orange, New Jersey with Dr. Rajal Patel​

At Yogi Dental Center, we believe everyone deserves access to exceptional dental care—without financial stress. That’s why we proudly offer flexible payment plans, easy financing solutions, and support with most major insurance plans. Whether you’re in need of preventive, restorative, or cosmetic dental treatment, we make it simple to manage the cost of your care.

Trusted Financing Options​

Flexible Payment Options​

To accommodate a wide range of financial situations, we accept several convenient payment methods:

  • Cash
  • Checks
  • Visa
  • MasterCard
  • Discover
  • American Express
  • CareCredit
  • Springstone Financing
  • Cherry
  • Sunbit
  • Happen
  • NerdWallet

You deserve a healthy, beautiful smile—and we’re here to help make that possible with payment options that work for you.

Interest-Free & Extended Payment Plans with CareCredit​

We proudly offer CareCredit, a healthcare credit card designed specifically to help you pay for out-of-pocket dental expenses not covered by insurance.

Benefits of CareCredit include:

  • No-interest and extended payment plans
  • No upfront costs
  • No prepayment penalties
  • Quick and easy application process
  • Options available for a wide range of credit histories

Apply now online or speak with our friendly team—we’ll be happy to assist you with the application process.

Flexible Financing with Alphaeon Credit​

Alphaeon Credit is a healthcare credit card that helps cover treatments not fully covered by insurance, with plans designed to fit a range of budgets.

Benefits of Alphaeon Credit include:

  • Credit lines up to $25,000 for treatments over $250
  • Promotional interest-free periods available
  • Reusable for future treatments, again and again
  • Instant pre-qualification results
  • Soft credit check to pre-qualify—no impact to your credit score

Apply now online—checking your rate uses a soft inquiry and won't affect your credit score.

Personal Loan Financing with Happen​

Happen (formerly LendingClub) offers personal loans that can be used to help cover the cost of dental treatment.

Benefits of Happen include:

  • Loan amounts from $1,000 to $75,000
  • Flexible terms from 24 to 84 months
  • Funding as fast as 24 hours after approval
  • Rates typically lower than credit card APRs
  • Checking your rate won't affect your credit score

Check your rate online—it won't affect your credit score.

Payment Plans with Cherry​

Cherry offers simple payment plans for dental treatment with a fast, soft-credit-check application.

Benefits of Cherry include:

  • ~35-second application with no impact to your credit score
  • Industry-leading ~90% approval rate
  • Zero-interest Pay-in-4 plan for every approved applicant
  • No hidden, deferred, or compounding interest
  • Immediate funding upon approval

Find Yogi Dental Center on Cherry's provider finder to apply—search "Yogi Dental" to get started.

Pay Over Time with Sunbit​

Sunbit offers flexible payment plans with no hidden fees, approved in seconds for a wide range of credit types.

Benefits of Sunbit include:

  • Application completed in seconds, right at checkout
  • 87% approval rate, starting at a 500 credit score
  • No hard credit check—won't affect your credit score
  • No late fees or hidden costs, ever
  • No-interest plans available up to 24 months

Find Yogi Dental Center on Sunbit's directory to pre-qualify—search "Yogi Dental" to get started.
