Personalized Dental Care Rooted in Trust and Expertise​

At Yogi Dental, your smile is our purpose.​

Founded with a clear mission—to deliver exceptional dental care in a warm, stress-free environment—Yogi Dental has become a trusted destination for families and individuals in Denville and surrounding communities. Our practice is led by highly skilled and compassionate dental professionals who are dedicated to making your visit as comfortable and informative as possible.

What Sets Us Apart:

  • Advanced digital technology for diagnostics and treatment
  • Gentle, judgment-free care for all ages
  • Focus on patient education and preventative care
  • A relaxing environment with a friendly, professional team

We believe a great smile starts with great communication. At Yogi Dental, you’ll always be heard, respected, and cared for like family.

Our Approach

The Procedure for a Check-up at YogiDental​

Learn More
01

Make an Appointment​

Easily schedule your visit online or by phone at your convenience. Our friendly staff will confirm your details and answer any questions.

02

Meet The Dentist​

You'll be warmly welcomed and introduced to your dentist, who will review your dental history and listen to your concerns.

03

General Dental Check Up​

We’ll perform a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, and jaw, using advanced tools like digital X-rays and 3D imaging if needed.

04

Prescribe & Payment​

Based on your exam, we’ll provide personalized treatment recommendations and help you understand costs, insurance, and payment options.

Our Doctors

Team Of Professionals​

Meet Dr. Rajal Patel DDS​

Meet Dr. Rajal J. Patel – Experienced, Compassionate Care

Dr. Rajal J. Patel has been creating healthy, confident smiles since 1990. A proud graduate of New York University’s College of Dentistry, Dr. Patel brings decades of expertise to every patient she treats. With advanced training in orthodontics, Dr. Patel is certified in Invisalign clear aligners, nitrous oxide sedation, and all aspects of general dentistry. She collaborates closely with a trusted network of oral surgeons and periodontists, ensuring seamless, comprehensive care for even the most complex cases.

Outside the office, Dr. Patel is an avid reader, enjoys cooking, and is actively involved in local community service. Her dedication to lifelong learning and her warm, patient-centered approach make her a favorite among patients of all ages.

Meet Dr. Niraj Patel, D.D.S.​

Meet Dr. Niraj – Your Smile Architect

Welcome! I’m Dr. Niraj, and if you’re looking for a dentist who blends artistry, science, and a dash of humor, you’ve come to the right place. I began my journey at New York University, earning both my undergraduate and dental degrees—go Violets! My passion for dental innovation led me to achieve Associate Fellowship status with the American Academy of Implant Dentistry in 2015. Since then, I’ve trained at leading institutions like the Spear and Zimmer Institutes, mastering everything from implant placement and bone grafting to sinus lifts, endodontics, and restorative design. Dentistry isn’t just about teeth—it’s about your whole well-being. I take a comprehensive approach, evaluating jaw joints, bite alignment, and the root causes of tooth wear. Whether I’m placing implants, crafting crowns, performing root canals, or perfecting smiles with Invisalign and veneers, every detail matters. I’m proud to work alongside Dr. Patel, combining our expertise to detect and treat cavities and gum disease early. Together, we’re committed to keeping your smile healthy and beautiful for years to come. I use advanced 3D imaging with Cone Beam CT technology—think of it as GPS for your mouth—to ensure precise diagnosis and treatment. The result? Comfortable care, lasting results, and a smile you’ll love to show off. Whether you’re here for a checkup or a complete smile makeover, I promise to make your experience as comfortable—and maybe even as enjoyable—as possible.

High Standards​

We deliver top-quality, personalized dental care with advanced cosmetic and restorative treatments. In emergencies, we prioritize prompt care to ensure your comfort and long-term oral health.

Education & Prevention​

We believe in preventive care and patient education for optimal dental health. Our focus includes thorough exams, oral cancer checks, x-rays, cleanings, sealants, and reviewing medical history to ensure total health and a beautiful smile.

Uncompromising Safety​

We prioritize infection control by strictly following ADA, OSHA, and CDC guidelines. Our sterilization and cross-contamination protocols ensure a safe, clean environment for every patient and team member at all times.

Training & Expertise​

We are a skilled dental team committed to your healthy smile. Through ongoing education and membership in professional associations, we stay updated on the latest techniques, products, and equipment to provide you with state-of-the-art dental care.

A Positive Experience​

We build trust by treating each patient as unique. Understanding dental visit anxieties, we strive to create a relaxing, positive experience. Our dedicated team provides personalized care to make every visit comfortable and pleasant for you.
